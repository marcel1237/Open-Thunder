/* ============================================================
* TECHNOLOGY: DARK VOLT
* Purpose: Early-Boot Hardware-Level Browser Initialization
* ============================================================ */

Concept:
Dark Volt is designed to bypass the standard Desktop Environment (DE) startup sequence.
Instead of waiting for GNOME, KDE, or even a Display Manager (GDM/SDDM), Dark Volt
targets the "Early User Space" phase.

Operational Strategy:
1. Target: Systemd high-priority unit (DefaultDependencies=no).
2. Backend: Qt EGLFS (Direct DRM/KMS access).
3. Priority: After local-fs.target, before sysinit.target.

Mathematic Gain:
By eliminating the DE startup (T_de), the browser availability (T_avail)
shifts from T_boot + 20s to T_boot + 2s.
T_avail = T_kernel + T_drivers + T_thunder_init
